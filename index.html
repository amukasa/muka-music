About Muka

Muka brings together music discovery and everyday productivity in one beautifully designed experience. Bypassing generic algorithms, Muka combines expert curation, AI-powered discovery, and seamless Apple Music integration with practical tools designed to help you get more from your day.

From finding your next favorite artist to organizing your schedule, staying focused, tracking your activity, and checking the weather, Muka puts the tools you use every day in one place.

Key Features

🎵 Curated Global Music

Access exclusive playlists, explore deep music stories, and discover world-class tracks from across the globe.

✨ AI Playlist Generation

Instantly create personalized playlists tailored to your mood, taste, activity, or vibe.

🔍 Music Recognition

Identify songs playing around you and save them straight to your Apple Music library.

📅 Tasks & Calendar

Organize your daily schedule, stay on top of to-dos, and streamline your routine.

🎯 Focus & Productivity

Block distracting apps and stay dialed in with dedicated focus sessions designed to help you get things done.

🏃 Fitness & Step Tracking

Track your daily steps, monitor your activity, and stay motivated to keep moving.

☀️ Weather & Widgets

View live daily high and low forecasts and add customizable Home Screen widgets for one-tap access to your favorite tools.
